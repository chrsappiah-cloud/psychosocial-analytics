GUIDELINE 5.1.1(v) — ACCOUNT DELETION
----------------------------------------------------------------------

Resolution:
- Account deletion is implemented in Settings → Account → Delete Account.
- Two-step confirmation is required before permanent deletion.
- Deletion permanently removes the user profile and all local data (assessments, clients, uploads) on the device.

How to test:
1. Public Access tab: sign in with any email.
2. Go to Settings tab.
3. Scroll to Account section.
4. Tap Delete Account → Continue → Delete Account.
5. App signs out and all local data is erased.

Screenshot #8 in the updated set shows the Delete Account button. A screen recording demonstrating the full flow is noted in App Review Information for future submissions.
